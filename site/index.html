MedCARAG: Context-Aware Medical RAG Benchmark and Full-Text Index

MedCARAG (Medical Context-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is an open benchmark, full-text index and toolkit for context-aware medical RAG. It tests whether a medical RAG system retrieves the context that makes a clinical finding safe to repeat (the population, the comparator and the caveats from the same study) as well as the passage that matches the question.

Code and benchmark on GitHub Indexes (229 GB) Identifiers and MeSH data Archived on Zenodo (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23934643)

Why context-aware retrieval

A clinical question usually matches the Results of a study. The passages that make that result interpretable, such as the population and comparator in Methods and the caveats in Discussion, sit elsewhere in the same article and do not match the question, so direct RAG leaves them out and the generator receives a decontextualised fact. MedCARAG measures this at full-text scale and tests retrieval that conditions on, and returns more of, the documents behind a match.

Key results

  • Direct RAG is poor at qualified evidence. BM25 top-10 finds an article on the right topic for 60.6% of 2,000 clinical queries, but returns a rate together with its population for 14.2%.
  • Context-aware retrieval roughly doubles readiness to 27.9%, and stays 5.8 points ahead of BM25 given the same number of passages.
  • The section has little influence. Random passages of the retrieved articles do as well as passages chosen by section type.
  • The gain partly reaches the answer. With a Gemma 4 26B-A4B generator, answers citing a rate from the right study rise from 21% to 30%; caveats are almost never passed on.

Frequently asked questions

What is context-aware medical RAG?

Medical retrieval-augmented generation that retrieves the documents behind a match, not only the best-matching passage, so the generator also sees whom a result applies to and what limits it.

How is MedCARAG different from MedRAG and MIRAGE?

MIRAGE and the MedRAG toolkit benchmark medical question answering over PubMed titles and abstracts and score the final answer. MedCARAG is a separate resource built on full text that scores whether the retrieved evidence carries its qualifying context, and releases full-text indexes, controls and per-query rows.

Which retrieval methods are included?

Direct BM25 RAG (sparse), document conditioning (page), sibling re-scoring (bleed), same-document expansion (context) and their combinations (pagectx, bleedctx), plus dense (bge-small-en-v1.5), hybrid and re-ranked modes.
